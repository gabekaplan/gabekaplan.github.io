### Pesto Sauce

**Ingredients**

- 2 cups basil leaves (pressed/packed)
- 5 tbsp pine nuts (cheaper alternative: walnuts)\*
- 3 large cloves garlic
- ½ cup olive oil
- salt to taste (I use Herbamare from Costco)
- Trader Joe's nutritional yeast to taste (start with ~3-4 tbsp)
> \* Note: The Costco pack of pine nuts is very reasonably priced compared to typical grocery stores.

**Instructions**

1. Blend all ingredients together in a food processor until desired consistency is reached
2. Add further ingredient quantities to taste and blend

### Almond Butter Breakfast Smoothie

Serving Size: 2 cups (473 mL)

**Ingredients**

- 1 cup strawberries, frozen
- 1 banana, frozen or fresh
- ¾ cup almond milk, vanilla or plain
- ¼ cup (1 scoop) vanilla protein powder

**Instructions**

1. Add all ingredients to blender
2. Blend for 1-2 minutes until all desired consistency is reached

### Ramen Stir Fry

*Fun Fact: This happens to be my first original recipe!*

**Ingredients**

- Gai Lan (alternative: Broccoli)
- Bok-choy
- Green Beans
- Spring Onions
- Carrots
- Peppers
- Ramen Noodles (I use Costco Rice Ramen)

**Honey Garlic Sauce**

- Soy Sauce
- Minced Garlic
- Honey
- Corn Starch

1. For sauce: mix equal parts soy sauce and honey, heat in microwave.
2. Add in minced garlic and 1 tbsp corn starch, mix well.

**Instructions**

1. Add oil to pan and turn on to Medium-High
2. Seperately cook ramen noodles in a pot
3. Add green beans, gai lan, bok-choy, and carrots to pan
4. Cook for 20-25 minutes
5. Add in peppers and spring onion and cook for ~5 more minutes
6. Add cooked ramen noodles to pan
7. Add sauce ingredients together and mix well, then add into stir fry, cook for additional 5 minutes

*Optional*

- While cooking vegetables, cut boneless chicken thighs cut into small pieces
- Fry chicken on Medium-High for 20-25 minutes in honey garlic sauce instead of oil
- Mix chicken with the vegetable stir fry and enjoy!

### Pesto Chicken

**Ingredients**

- boneless skinless chicken tighs **or** chicken breasts
- 1 pack of Linguine or Spaghetti pasta
- ⅓ - ¼ cup pine nuts
- **{{< component >}}pesto sauce{{< /component >}}**

**Instructions**

1. Boil and strain pasta as described on the package
2. Cut chicken into bite-sized pieces
3. Fry chicken on medium-high until cooked (~15 minutes)
4. Add pine nuts to pan to toast them
5. Add pasta to pan and fry for 1 minute
6. Add pesto sauce to pan and mix well

### Zuccini Bread

**Ingredients**

- 3 eggs, extra large
- ¼ cup oil
- 3 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 cups puréed zuccini (~2 zuccinis)
- 2 cups flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 3 tsp cinnamon
- *Optional: cocoa powder*

**Instructions**

1. Preheat oven to 350 °F
2. Blend zuccinis in a food processor
3. Mix dry ingredients in a large mixing bowl (flour, salt, baking powder, and cinnamon)
4. Add eggs and oil to bowl and mix well
5. Add puréed zuccini and vanilla extract to bowl and mix until even
6. *If desired, add cocoa powder and mix/swirl into marbled pattern*
7. Pour into a greased baking tin and bake for 1 hour

### Buni's Orange Blueberry Loaf

*This recipe is from a Holocaust survivor, the grandmother of a friend of mine.*

**Ingredients**

- 1 ½ cups sugar
- 2 sticks sweet margerine
- 4 eggs, large
- 2 ½ cups flour
- 1 tsp baking powder, mixed with a dash of salt
- juice and zest from 1 large Navel orange\*
- ¼ - 1 cup milk
- 1 pint blueberries
> \* Note: The original recipes calls for ⅓ cup orange juice and 2 tsp orange rind

**Instructions**

1. Preheat oven to 325 °F.
2. Beat margerine with sugar and milk.
3. Add eggs one at a time and beat well.
4. Add orange juice and zest
5. Add flour slowly, stir until combined and smooth.
6. Add flour-coated blueberries to the batter (flour prevents sinking).
7. Add extra milk as needed if batter is dry.
8. Pour batter into greased loaf tin, bake approx. 45 minutes.

### The Best Bread Machine Challah

[*Recipe from online*](https://www.food.com/recipe/the-best-bread-machine-challah-142387?scale=2)

Note: This recipe yields 2 pounds of dough or 2 medium-size challahs. They are some of the
best I have ever had. My machine calls for the wet ingredients and salt to be
placed in the pan first, followed by the remaining dry ingredients. Using a
finger, make a well in the dry ingredients and add the yeast. Press dough cycle.

Servings: 3 medium challahs

**Ingredients**

- 5 large egg yolks
- ⅓ cup oil
- 1 ½ cups water
- 4 ¼ - 5 cups all-purpose or bread flour
- ½ cup (plus 1 generous tablespoon) sugar
- 1 ⅛ teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon active dry yeast
- 1 large egg, lightly beaten
- sesame seeds or poppy seeds

**Instructions**

1. Place the egg yolks, oil, water, flour, sugar, salt, and yeast in the bread
   machine pan, according to manufacturer’s directions. Process the ingredients
   in the dough cycle (90 minutes). Remove immediately when the machine beeps.
2. Preheat oven to 350 °F. Divide the dough in half. Divide each piece into
   3 sections.
3. Roll each section into a long strand. Lay the three strands in front of you.
   Pinch the top ends together. Braid the dough and pinch the bottom ends
   together. Place on parchment-lined or lightly greased baking sheets. Repeat
   with remaining dough. Let the challot rise covered, for 30 minutes
4. Brush the challah with beaten egg. Sprinkle with sesame seeds, poppy seeds,
   or a mixture of both. Bake for 30 minutes. Cool on a wire rack.
